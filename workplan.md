# Homemade Pizza Dough

Manuel’s simple pizza dough recipe — makes **2 medium pizzas**.

## Ingredients

- 300 g flour
- 195 ml lukewarm water
- 1 tsp instant dry yeast (~3 g)
- 1 tsp salt (~6 g)
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 120g mozzarella
- Canned peeled tomatos 
- Salt and oregano
- Basil leaves or toppings of choosing

## Instructions

1. **Mix:** Combine the flour, yeast and salt. Add the water and olive oil, then mix into a rough dough.
2. **Knead:** Knead for **8–10 minutes**, until smooth and elastic. If it’s sticky, add just a little flour.
3. **Let rise:** Place the dough in a bowl, cover it and leave somewhere warm for **1–2 hours**, until roughly doubled in size.
4. **Shape:** Divide into two balls, rest for **15 minutes**, then stretch into pizza bases.

Your dough is now ready for your favourite pizza toppings!
